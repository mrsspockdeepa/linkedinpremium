The Challenge

Premium underdelivers because it sits adjacent to hiring, not inside it

Subscribers report low InMail response rates, dismiss the “Top Applicant” badge as theatre, and frequently cancel within two months. The intuitive response — ship more job-seeker features — misses three structural problems.

🎯

Wrong customer focus

Job seekers are inventory, not customers. Recruiters and Recruiter Corporate seats generate materially more revenue per user than Premium Career.

📊

Missing feedback loop

LinkedIn cannot tell users why they were rejected or which skills correlate with interviews because it has no outcome data from the ATS.

🔗

Partner-dependent fixes

Verified passports inside Workday and autonomous agents both require multi-year enterprise negotiations LinkedIn doesn’t control.

“LinkedIn cannot improve hiring outcomes until it can measure them, and it cannot build lasting differentiation until it produces value recruiters will pay more for.”
The thesis
The Strategy

Three phases, one wedge

Rather than ship a long roadmap in parallel, the strategy sequences three phases. Each phase is gated on the previous phase producing specific evidence. The wedge — the Application Outcome Tracker — is the smallest possible feature that generates the proprietary dataset everything else depends on.

Phase 1 · Q1–Q2

Earn the data

Build the outcome feedback loop. Fix trust with transparent billing. Deliver a free recruiter insights layer that makes LinkedIn more valuable to the paying side.

Phase 2 · Q3–Q4

Earn the trust

Use six months of outcome data to ship calibrated matching and targeted learning paths. Limited-beta referral requests with one design-partner employer.

Phase 3 · Year 2+

Earn the partners

Once Phase 1 data proves LinkedIn improves hiring outcomes, ATS vendors have a reason to integrate. Verified passports become negotiable. Not before.

Phase 1 Features

Three things ship in Phase 1. Nothing else is funded until they produce evidence.

Each feature has a primary success metric and a pre-committed kill condition. Tap any feature to expand functional requirements and details.

1
Application Outcome Tracker The wedge · Greenlit Q1 · The data collector that unlocks everything

A lightweight candidate-side tool that captures the outcome of every application a user submits — whether through LinkedIn or not. Users mark each application as Applied, Screened, Interviewed, Rejected, or Offered, with optional rejection reason. Over time, LinkedIn accumulates the first real dataset connecting profile attributes to hiring outcomes.

Why it’s the wedge: calibrated matching, honest match scores, skill gap recommendations, recruiter insights — everything else in the strategy depends on this dataset existing.

  • FR-1.1System SHALL present a simple outcome tracker on every LinkedIn-initiated application, with optional manual entry for external applications.
  • FR-1.2Outcome states: Applied, Auto-rejected, Screened, Phone screen, Onsite, Offered, Accepted, Withdrawn.
  • FR-1.3System SHALL prompt users once weekly with a single lightweight question, not require proactive entry.
  • FR-1.4Rejected applications SHALL allow free-text plus structured taxonomy (Lacked skill, Overqualified, Filled internally, No reason).
  • FR-1.5System SHALL aggregate outcomes anonymously and surface patterns: “Candidates with skill X reached phone screen 2.4× more often.”
  • FR-1.6Aggregated insights SHALL require ≥50 outcome events per role-company slice before surfacing.
  • FR-1.7Outcome data SHALL persist for the user even after Premium cancellation. The user owns their data.
Primary metric: 2,000 role × company outcome slices with ≥50 data points within 90 days. Kill condition: <400 slices → pause Phase 2.
2
Subscription transparency & one-click cancel Greenlit Q1 · Trust repair · The cheapest fix for the loudest complaint

Pure trust repair. The auto-renewal trap is the top complaint and the cheapest fix. Removes dark patterns that compound sentiment damage every month, without touching pricing.

  • FR-2.1System SHALL provide one-click cancel from main Premium settings, reachable in ≤2 clicks from any Premium surface.
  • FR-2.2System SHALL offer a pause option (up to 6 months) preserving saved searches, application history, and outcome data.
  • FR-2.3Each billing event SHALL include a usage report showing which features the user actually engaged with that month.
  • FR-2.4System SHALL proactively notify users with zero engagement in 30 days, offering to pause.
  • FR-2.5System SHALL NOT introduce new retention friction, dark patterns, or multi-step cancel flows. Enforced by design review.
  • FR-2.6Application Outcome Tracker data SHALL remain accessible to cancelled users in read-only form.
Primary metric: Forgot-to-cancel refund requests drop 50%. Guardrail: voluntary churn up <10% vs. baseline.
3
Recruiter Outcome Insights Greenlit Q2 · Recruiter-side value · The feature that justifies the strategy to revenue

The feature that makes the strategy two-sided. As the Outcome Tracker accumulates data, a derived dashboard surfaces anonymized insights to the paying customer: which sources produce candidates who pass screens, which JDs attract qualified applicants, where pipelines drop off.

Why it matters more than Premium Career features: Recruiter Corporate is the profitable product. Anything that makes a Recruiter seat more valuable is worth 3–5× more in revenue than the equivalent Premium Career uplift. Bundled free into existing Recruiter subscriptions to improve retention.

  • FR-3.1System SHALL expose an aggregated outcome dashboard to Recruiter Lite and Corporate seats showing pipeline quality per posted role.
  • FR-3.2Per-role metrics: candidates sourced, % reaching phone screen, % onsite, % offered, median time-in-stage, peer benchmarks.
  • FR-3.3System SHALL derive metrics exclusively from the Outcome Tracker. No ATS integration required.
  • FR-3.4All candidate-level data SHALL be k-anonymized with k≥10. No individual outcome is ever exposed.
  • FR-3.5System SHALL allow recruiters to compare JD language across roles, flagging JDs that correlate with poor candidate quality.
  • FR-3.6Dashboard SHALL be bundled with existing Recruiter subscriptions at no additional charge.
Primary metric: Recruiter Corporate renewal rate +3 pts within 12 months. Kill condition: recruiter weekly active <15% after 6 months.
What Comes Next

Phase 2 & 3 features are designed but not funded

Each requires Phase 1 to produce specific evidence before it enters design. This is the discipline the strategy depends on.

🎯

Calibrated Match Quality

A successor to “Top Applicant” trained on real outcome data. Ships only if the model achieves AUC ≥0.70 on held-out validation.

🎓

Targeted Learning Paths

When outcome data reveals which skills correlate with rejection, a specific LinkedIn Learning course closes the loop from diagnosis to remediation.

🤝

Referral Requests (closed beta)

Structured warm-intro requests, but only as a closed beta with one design-partner employer. No general rollout until recipient sentiment is proven.

🤖

AI Assistance, not Agents

Human-initiated “tailor my resume” and interview prep. No autonomous agents, no auto-drafted applications — the economics and recruiter blast radius are unacceptable.

🔐

Verified Candidate Passport

Blocked pending a signed LOI from at least one top-5 ATS vendor. No engineering headcount until that LOI exists.

⚠️

Kill conditions on every feature

Pre-committed thresholds at which each feature rolls back. Aspirations are nice; exit ramps are necessary.

Prototypes

Two prototypes, two sides of the marketplace

The first shows the candidate-side wedge that produces the data. The second shows the recruiter-side value that the same data unlocks. Together they illustrate why the strategy bets on a dataset rather than a feature.

linkedin.com/premium/tracker

Candidate-side. Click the outcome buttons to mark an application’s status. Designed for under 30 seconds per week.

linkedin.com/recruiter/insights

Recruiter-side. Pipeline quality, JD scoring, source quality — all derived from anonymized candidate outcomes.

The meta-point

Features built on top of a proprietary outcome dataset become possible only once the dataset exists. The strategy is to ship the cheapest possible thing that produces it — and let the data earn the right to ship everything else.
